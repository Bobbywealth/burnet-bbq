Our Story

The Pitmaster's Promise

40+ Years of Excellence
Carlos Teixeira
Owner & Pitmaster

No CEOs. No Corporate. Just Real BBQ.

My name is Carlos Teixeira, and I am the owner of Burnet BBQ. At my restaurants there are no Regional Managers, no corporate oversight — just me, my three hands-on managers, and a commitment to doing things right.

I personally visit each of our five locations two to three times every single day. If something needs my attention, I'm just a phone call away. It's very rare that we receive complaints — but when we do, I address them personally to ensure 100% customer satisfaction.
